# Cookie Policy for VibePause

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the VibePause website at https://tanveer-exe.github.io/VibePause/ (the "Website") and the VibePause Windows application (the "App") use cookies and similar technologies. It is part of our Privacy Policy.

## In short

- The Website does not set any cookies.
- The Website does not use local storage, session storage, IndexedDB or similar technologies to store anything on your device.
- The App is a desktop program. It does not use cookies.
- We use no analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking tools, so there is nothing to ask your consent for and the Website shows no cookie banner.

## What this means for consent

Consent is needed for cookies and similar technologies that are not strictly necessary. Because the Website does not set any, no cookie consent is required. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.

## Third parties

The Website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We are not aware of GitHub setting cookies on this Website when you only view it, but GitHub controls its own hosting. The Website also links to other sites, such as GitHub, Ko-fi and SignPath. If you click through to them, they may set their own cookies under their own policies, which we do not control.

## Your browser settings

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so does not affect how the Website works.

## Contact us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/Tanveer-exe/VibePause/issues.
